# Privacy Policy — Sea Salt Scorekeeper

_Last updated: May 2, 2026_

Sea Salt Scorekeeper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game data (player names, scores, and game history) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and is never sent to any server.

The app does not use advertising or any third-party tracking services.

## Crash Reporting

The app uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to capture anonymous crash reports when the app encounters an unexpected error. These reports contain no personally identifiable information and are used solely to fix bugs. You can review Sentry's privacy policy at [sentry.io/privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy).
